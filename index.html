What does the Hub provide?

When are picks posted?

Plays may release at different points during the day as markets develop. Each post includes its timing and the information needed to evaluate it.

Will results be recapped?

Yes. The goal is a clear daily recap after relevant games are final.

No. Sports betting includes risk. Past results do not guarantee future outcomes. Only wager what you can afford to lose.
