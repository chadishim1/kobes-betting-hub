# Responsible-Gambling Disclosures

**Draft for legal/compliance review before publication.** Kobe's Betting Hub is an information and entertainment membership, not a sportsbook, gambling operator, financial adviser, or results guarantee. Counsel must confirm age, wording, links, and jurisdictions before launch.

## Core disclosure

**21+ only. Gambling involves risk. You can lose money, and no pick or past result guarantees a future outcome. Set time and money limits, never chase losses, and never bet money needed for essentials. Use only legal, regulated operators where you are physically located. Kobe's Betting Hub does not accept wagers or control sportsbook accounts.**

If gambling is causing financial, emotional, work, or relationship harm, stop and seek support. In the U.S., call or text **1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738)** or visit [NCPG help](https://www.ncpgambling.org/help-treatment/). This is not an emergency line. For immediate danger in the U.S., call 911 or call/text **988**.

Arizona residents can also call **1-800-NEXT-STEP (1-800-639-8783)**, text **NEXTSTEP to 53342**, or use [Arizona resources](https://gaming.az.gov/ResponsibleGaming) and [self-exclusion](https://gaming.az.gov/exclude).

## Short footer

**21+ | Gambling involves risk; never chase losses. No guaranteed results. U.S.: call/text 1-800-MY-RESET. Arizona: 1-800-NEXT-STEP or text NEXTSTEP to 53342. Immediate danger: 911 or 988.**

- “I agree to the Terms, recurring price/cadence, cancellation policy, and refund policy shown here.”

## Performance disclosure

“Past performance is not indicative of future results. Records use released lines and odds under the published methodology. Prices vary by location and operator. See the complete record, units risked, ROI, date range, exclusions, and correction policy.”

## Marketing and support rules

- No “lock,” “risk-free,” “guaranteed,” “can't lose,” wealth, bill-paying, debt-recovery, or loss-chasing claims.
- Never frame membership or wagers as investments, target minors/self-excluded people, glorify reckless stakes, or shame non-bettors.
- Place the short disclosure on performance promotions and link the full methodology. Show losses/corrections consistently.
- Operator bonuses require jurisdiction, terms, age, and affiliate-disclosure review.
- For gambling-harm requests: stop retention/sales language and promotions where possible. Send: “Thanks for telling us. We’ll help with your membership request. Confidential U.S. support: call/text 1-800-MY-RESET. Arizona: 1-800-NEXT-STEP or text NEXTSTEP to 53342. Immediate danger: 911 or 988.”
- Do not diagnose, ask losses, suggest a smaller bet, or use the interaction to retain the member.
