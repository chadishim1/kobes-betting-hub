# Kobe's Betting Hub - Project Plan

## Goal

Relaunch Kobe's paid betting community with a subscription website, a clear member experience, a reviewed pick-publishing workflow, daily recaps, and a repeatable marketing process.

## What we can build now

1. Brand, offer, website copy, and membership requirements.
2. Source/content inventory and pick-format templates.
3. Kobe writing-style guide and drafted customer messages.
4. Daily recap and release-summary templates.
5. Instagram content calendar, captions, and reusable creative briefs.
6. Requirements for the future payment, Discord, and social integrations.

## Decisions pending from Kobe

- Membership price, renewal cadence, refund/cancellation policy, and returning-member discount.
- Final Discord server/channel/role structure.
- Partner source list and any attribution rules.
- Who approves picks, customer messages, refunds, and access problems.
- Website scope beyond memberships.
- The approved Discord access/integration approach.

## Phases

### Phase 0 - Discovery and launch decisions

**Outcome:** a fixed first-version scope and all account owners identified.

- Complete `docs/MEETING_AGENDA.md` with Kobe.
- Fill in the source tracker and writing samples.
- Confirm the paid offer and launch date.
- Decide what is free, paid, and exclusive.

### Phase 1 - Membership foundation

**Outcome:** a website where a customer can understand the offer and join.

- Write the home page, membership page, FAQ, support, terms, privacy, and responsible-gambling copy.
- Choose payment provider and configure subscriptions.
- Define customer states: trial/discounted, active, past due, canceled, refunded.
- Define Discord role mapping once the Discord decision is made.

### Phase 2 - Content operations

**Outcome:** approved picks can be processed consistently, even manually at first.

- Create source list and attribution rules.
- Standardize pick, release-summary, and result-recap templates.
- Establish an approval queue and owner.
- Log every published pick with date, sport, line, source, result, and units.
- Trial the process before NFL season.

Manual-first package: see `docs/CONTENT_OPERATIONS.md`. It includes the intake and
approval workflow, canonical pick log, release-summary process, recap closeout, and
the explicit boundary against accessing partner Discord communities.

### Phase 3 - Member experience

**Outcome:** customers understand where to go, how to get help, and what they receive.

- Create channel map and access rules.
- Finalize welcome/impersonation-warning message.
- Draft returning-member relaunch announcement and discount offer.
- Build support replies and escalation process.

### Phase 4 - Marketing operations

**Outcome:** daily marketing can be produced and reviewed quickly.

- Build a content source inventory and permissions notes.
- Prepare one Reel brief, one feed graphic brief, and two Story briefs per day.
- Create caption and CTA library in Kobe's voice.
- Establish a review-and-publish cadence.

### Phase 5 - Integrations and automation

**Outcome:** automate only stable, approved workflows.

- Connect the payment system, website, and Discord according to the selected supported method.
- Add scheduled recap/release generation.
- Connect Instagram professional-account publishing through Meta's approved tools if used.
- Start with draft generation and approval; automate only repeatable low-risk actions.

## Launch criteria for the first paid version

- Membership offer, price, and policies are visible on the website.
- Customer can pay and receive a clear next step for access.
- Pick approval, posting, and tracking work reliably.
- Daily recap has a repeatable format and owner.
- Welcome/support messages are ready.
- Relaunch message and discount are ready and tested with a small group.
- Access, payment, and source procedures have an accountable owner.

## Simple operating flow

`approved source -> review -> Kobe/admin approval -> publish -> pick log -> result -> daily recap`

`website checkout -> subscription state -> member access -> onboarding -> support`
