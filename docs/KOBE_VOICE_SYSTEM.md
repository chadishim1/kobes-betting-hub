# Kobe Voice and Member Communications System

**Status:** Calibrated from a limited Kobe-authored sample set; expand and obtain Kobe's approval before treating it as final.

## Voice in one line

Short, casual, and straight to the point. Kobe writes like he is talking in the chat, not composing brand copy.

## Principles

1. Lead with the answer or next step.
2. Use very short sentences and ordinary chat language.
3. Be confident about the process, never a betting outcome.
4. Own mistakes plainly and correct them quickly.
5. Ask only for the minimum information needed. Never request passwords, login codes, full card numbers, security codes, or wallet credentials.
6. End support messages with an action, owner, or concrete update time.

## Observed style profile

The following patterns come from three clearly visible Kobe-authored Discord messages in the August 13, 2026 screen recording. The sample is enough to calibrate casual betting updates, but not enough to establish his support, billing, complaint, or promotional style.

| Element | Starting default |
|---|---|
| Greeting | Usually none in quick chat updates |
| Point of view | “I” only when Kobe is personally speaking; otherwise “we” |
| Length | Usually one line; roughly 3–9 words in observed samples |
| Capitalization | Sentence starts capitalized; otherwise casual lowercase |
| Punctuation | Often no ending punctuation |
| Results | Record first, then “yesterday,” then emoji |
| Betting talk | Plain shorthand such as “k’s” and “outs” |
| Emojis | Situational and sometimes repeated; 🙏 after a strong day, 😱😱 for excited surprise |
| Sign-off | None |

### Verified examples

- “5-1 yesterday 🙏”
- “2-0 yesterday 😱😱”
- “Cash burkes k’s and his outs look good”

Preserve the casual rhythm, but correct names, lines, odds, records, and material policy facts before sending. Do not copy the typos or slang of other Discord users; only messages visibly authored by Kobe count as evidence.

## Tone by situation

| Situation | Tone | Member need |
|---|---|---|
| Routine question | Fast and friendly | Direct answer and next step |
| Access/billing | Calm and practical | Safe data request and update timing |
| Upset member | Respectful, non-defensive | Acknowledgment and resolution path |
| Team mistake | Plain and accountable | Correct facts and next action |
| Promotion | Energetic but specific | Price, eligibility, deadline, terms, CTA |
| Picks/results | Clear and disciplined | Accurate line/result; no guarantees |
| Gambling concern | Supportive, nonjudgmental | Stop promotion and route to help |

## Language rules

For casual updates, prefer direct constructions such as “[RECORD] yesterday [EMOJI],” “Cash [PLAYER] [MARKET],” and “[PLAYER/MARKET] looks good.” For customer support, keep the same short rhythm while retaining the necessary action and timing.

Avoid:

- “Lock,” “can’t lose,” “free money,” “risk-free,” or “guaranteed winner.”
- False urgency or pressure.
- Blaming the member.
- Vague timing such as “soon” or “ASAP.”
- Claiming Kobe personally wrote or reviewed something unless he did.
- Encouraging anyone to chase losses or increase stakes to recover.

## Message formula

For routine questions: answer or acknowledge → action → expectation.

For incidents: acknowledge impact → verified facts → immediate action → next update time/timezone → resolution confirmation.

## Approval levels

- **Green — pre-approved:** An authorized owner may personalize verified fields and send.
- **Yellow — review required:** Discounts, policy interpretations, complaints, public incidents, responsible-gambling concerns, or substantial rewrites.
- **Red — Kobe/owner approval required:** New policy, public apology, disputed charge, legal/safety threat, media or partnership statement, or a message written as Kobe personally.

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Offer and policy facts are current.
- [ ] Names, dates, prices, links, codes, status, and timezone are verified.
- [ ] No outcome guarantee or loss-chasing language appears.
- [ ] No sensitive credentials or full payment information is requested.
- [ ] “I” is used only by Kobe or with his explicit approval.
- [ ] Casual messages are short and unpolished in rhythm, but all betting and policy facts remain accurate.
- [ ] Required approval is recorded.
- [ ] All placeholders are removed and promised timing is realistic.

## Ownership and review

Kobe approves the voice baseline. The support owner maintains templates; policy owners approve billing, refund, cancellation, and access language. Record an owner, version, approval date, and next review date for every approved template. Review monthly during launch, quarterly afterward, and after any policy change or preventable communication issue.

## Calibration

Give Kobe three pairs of drafts: routine support, promotion, and losing-day recap. Ask which sounds more natural and why. Record his exact preferences in the intake tracker, revise this guide, and obtain written baseline approval.
