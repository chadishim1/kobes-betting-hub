# Kobe Meeting Agenda

## Paid offer

- What is the monthly price?
- Is there a free trial, and if so, how long?
- Is the returning-member offer 30% or 40% off? First month only or longer?
- What exactly does a paid member receive?
- What is the cancellation/refund policy?

## Membership and website

- Does Kobe already own a domain? If yes, which one and where is it registered?
- What other project needs does the website have outside Betting Hub?
- Who owns the payment account and handles refunds/chargebacks?
- What name, logo, colors, and photos should the website use?

## Content and picks

- Which source accounts/communities are in scope for launch?
- What source credit or formatting rules apply to each?
- Which picks are free, paid, and exclusive?
- Who approves every pick at launch?
- What is the exact daily deadline/expectation for recaps?

## Discord

- Which server is the paid community?
- Who owns it and who is an admin?
- What channels and roles already exist?
- Which channels should be paid-only?
- What is the desired welcome message and support path?
- What supported integration route should be used after reviewing the options?

## Marketing

- Which Instagram account will be used?
- Which source accounts can supply content?
- What is the daily posting goal and who approves content?
- What existing Reels, graphics, captions, and CTA styles should we imitate?

## Launch

- What date are we targeting?
- Who will test checkout, access, welcome messages, and the pick workflow?
- What makes the first paid launch successful after 30 days?
